What I'm working on

A company that brings together agentic systems and knowledge management, tools and methods for working with agents, and studies of user experience applied to AI.

CompanyBeing founded

Longward

A company to build products that help communities keep and develop what they learn together, using AI without erasing the people who produced the knowledge. Each contribution stays linked to its origin and serves whoever comes next.

ToolOpen source soon

AIKM

A local knowledge manager for people who work with AI. It stores notes, prompts, skills, and standards with the origin of each item and publishes approved copies to projects without changing the original. What you learn with agents in one project carries over to the next.

A method for building software with agents. Each task becomes a contract with an objective, a scope, and verification, and what each cycle teaches goes back into the method itself. AIKM was built with it.
