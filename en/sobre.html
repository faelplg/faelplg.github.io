Profile

I'm a software engineer specialized in UX and, more recently, AI. I started in front-end and full stack development, co-founded two companies, and spent more than ten years in technology leadership roles, the last five as CTO of Aurum Software.

At Aurum, I led a technology organization that grew to 50 people, covering product, engineering, cloud, data, security, and internal IT. Today I'm founding a new business and building my own projects through fael.tech.
